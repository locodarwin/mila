# Calendar feeds

A calendar feed shows your tasks in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. Tasks with a due date show as events, all day unless they have a time, and each links back to its task.

A feed only reads. It never changes anything in Mila.

## Making a feed

Go to Settings, Calendar feeds, or choose Subscribe in a calendar app at the top of a project or saved view. Then choose what to show:

- My tasks: open tasks assigned to you, and your My Day
- A project: its open tasks with a due date
- A saved view: its tasks with a due date. The feed follows the view's filter, field conditions included, but not its sort or grouping. That includes In my My Day, which means the tasks in your own My Day, and Has a due date

Choose Make a feed. This needs a sign-in from the last 10 minutes, with a passkey if you have one. If yours is older, Mila says so and offers Sign in again, which brings you back here.

## Using the link

Mila then shows the feed's link, and this is the only time it shows it. Copy it now.

Choose Open in a calendar app, or paste the link where your calendar subscribes to one. In Google Calendar, that is Other calendars, From URL.

Calendar apps look for changes every hour or so, so a change in Mila can take that long to appear.

Anyone with the link can see these tasks, so keep it to yourself.

## Managing feeds

Settings, Calendar feeds lists your feeds in the workspace picked under Workspace in Settings, with when each was made and last read. Revoke stops a link working at once, and takes a second click to confirm. You can have up to 100 feeds.

A feed shows what you can see when your calendar app reads it. So leaving the workspace, or losing a project as a guest, ends what it shows. Guests can make feeds too, and theirs show only the projects shared with them.

If an instance admin signs you out everywhere, your feeds end as well. So does deleting your account.
