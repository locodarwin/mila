# Projects

A project holds a body of work: its tasks, its statuses and its wiki. Projects belong to a workspace, and everyone in the workspace except guests sees all of them.

## Making a project

- Choose the plus beside Projects in the sidebar, type a name and press `Enter`.
- On the Projects page, reached with `g` then `p`, type in New project.
- From a [template](templates.md), with From a template on the Projects page.

The Projects page lists each project with how many open tasks it has, with archived projects below.

To reorder projects in the sidebar, focus one and use `Alt+Up` and `Alt+Down`.

The arrow beside Projects in the sidebar folds the list away, and each device remembers whether it is folded. The plus beside it opens the list again to add a project. A collapsed sidebar has no fold, and its lists always show.

## A project's menu

Right-click a project in the sidebar, on the Projects page, or its name at the top of its own page, for Open, Open in a new tab, Open its wiki, Rename, Color and icon, Share with guests, Subscribe in a calendar app, Save as a template, Copy link, Archive and Delete. An archived project has Unarchive instead of Archive. You see only what your role lets you do. See [Right-click menus](menus.md).

## A project's page

The header has the project's icon and color, its name, and its tools. Click the name to rename it. Click the icon to change the icon or color. A renamed project also renames its wiki's main page, and links to that page follow.

A project has three tabs. Tasks shows its tasks, Project Wiki shows [its wiki](wiki.md), and Time shows [the time logged](time-tracking.md#time-on-a-project-itself) on it, where you can log time to the project itself. It opens on whichever tab you had open last on this device.

On the Tasks tab you also get:

- a description, under the header
- List, Board, Calendar, Table and Timeline buttons. See [Layouts](layouts.md)
- Edit statuses
- Save as a view, the bookmark button, which puts this project's current layout in the sidebar as a [saved view](saved-views.md)
- Save as a template. See [Templates](templates.md)
- Subscribe in a calendar app. See [Calendar feeds](calendar-feeds.md)

On either tab there are also:

- Share with guests, for owners and admins in a shared workspace. See [People and roles](people-and-roles.md#sharing-a-project-with-a-guest)
- Archive, which takes the project out of the sidebar and lists and keeps everything. See [The trash and the archive](trash-and-archive.md)
- Delete

## Statuses

Each project has its own statuses, changed with Edit statuses at the top of the project. A new project starts with Backlog, To do, In progress, Done and Canceled.

In the editor you can rename a status, change its kind, delete it, or add one. Every status belongs to one of five kinds: Backlog, To do, In progress, Done or Canceled. So you could have "Waiting on client" as a kind of In progress, or "Shipped" as a kind of Done.

The kind decides what a status means:

- Moving a task to a Done or Canceled status completes it, and moving it back reopens it.
- Ticking a task's box moves it to the project's first Done status.
- A filter on Status offers each kind, covering every project's statuses of that kind, and the projects' own statuses by name.

Deleting a status moves its tasks to another of the same kind, or to another status if it was the last of its kind. A project keeps at least one status.

On a board whose columns are the statuses, right-click a column's heading for New task here, Rename the status, Its kind, Move left, Move right and Delete the status.

Tasks in the Inbox have no status.
