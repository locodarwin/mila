# Your first project

This walk takes about ten minutes. It uses a made-up project, repainting a bedroom, and touches the parts of Mila you will use most. You can delete it all afterward.

## 1. Make a project

Press `g` then `p` to go to Projects, or use the plus beside Projects in the sidebar. Type `Paint the bedroom` as the new project's name and press `Enter`.

The project opens. It starts with five statuses (Backlog, To do, In progress, Done and Canceled) and three tabs: Tasks, Project Wiki and Time.

## 2. Add tasks

Press `q` to open quick add, and type:

```
Buy paint fri !high
```

The chips under the field show what Mila understood: due Friday, high priority. Press `Enter`. Quick add puts the task in the project on screen, and the line under the field tells you so.

Add two more, using the same key:

```
Prep the walls sat
Paint the walls sun
```

You can also type a title into Add a task at the top of the list. [Quick add](quick-add.md) lists everything it can read.

## 3. Open a task

Press `j` and `k` to move up and down the list, or click a row. Press `Enter` on Buy paint, or click its title. The task opens in a panel beside the list.

Write `Eggshell finish, two gallons` in the notes. Changes save as you make them, so there is no Save button.

Under Checklist, add `Rollers` and `Tape`. A checklist is for small steps. Subtasks are for steps that need their own dates or people.

Press `Escape` to close the panel.

## 4. Make one task wait for another

Painting has to wait for the prep. Open Paint the walls, go to Dependencies, and under Blocked by choose Add a task it waits for. Type `Prep` and pick the task.

Paint the walls now shows a Blocked mark in the list. The mark goes when Prep the walls is done. See [Dependencies](dependencies.md).

## 5. See it another way

At the top of the project, choose Board. Each status is a column. Drag Buy paint to In progress.

Choose Calendar to see the tasks on their due dates, or Timeline to see the arrow between the two that depend on each other. Back on List, you are where you started. See [Layouts](layouts.md).

Right-click any task for a menu of what you can do to it, and hold `Ctrl` as you click to select several and change them together. See [Right-click menus](menus.md) and [Selecting several tasks](selecting-tasks.md).

## 6. Plan your day

On a task in the list, press `t`. This adds it to My Day, your own list for today. Press `g` then `d` to go there.

Nobody else sees what you picked, and picking a task changes nothing about the task. See [Inbox, My Day and Upcoming](daily-lists.md).

## 7. Finish something

Press `x` on a task, or tick its box. It moves out of the open tasks. Press `Ctrl+Z` to take that back.

## 8. Write something down

Open the project's Project Wiki tab. Its main page is named after the project. Write the paint color and where you bought it. See [The wiki](wiki.md).

## 9. Bring someone in

Your Personal workspace is just for you, so sharing starts with a shared workspace.

1. Open the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar and choose New workspace.
2. Go to Settings, People, type an address under Invite people, choose a role and choose Send invitation.

[People and roles](people-and-roles.md) explains the roles, and how to share a single project with a guest.

A project stays in the workspace where you made it. To bring tasks across, [export them as CSV and import that file](exports-and-imports.md) into the new workspace, or start the shared work there.

## 10. Clean up

Open the project and choose the trash button in its header, or right-click the project in the sidebar and choose Delete. A toast offers Undo. After that the project waits in the [trash](trash-and-archive.md) for 30 days.
