# Using Mila

This guide is for people who use Mila: someone meeting it for the first time, someone running a workspace, and someone invited to one project as a guest. It describes the app as it works today.

Read it from start to finish, or go straight to what you need. Each page stands on its own and links to the others.

## Where to start

- New to Mila? Read [What Mila is](what-is-mila.md), then follow [Your first project](first-project.md).
- Invited as a guest? Read [If you are a guest](guests.md).
- Running a workspace? Read [People and roles](people-and-roles.md), then [Notifications](notifications.md).

## How this guide writes things

Names of screens and buttons are written as the app shows them. "Settings, Preferences" means the Preferences page in Settings. Settings lists its pages in three sections: You; Workspace, for the workspace picked at its head; and This Mila, for instance admins. Each page has an icon beside its name. Picking a workspace there changes only Settings, not the workspace you have open. Your name at the foot of the sidebar opens Settings, and on a phone so does the gear in the top bar.

Keys are shown as they come out of the box. You can change most of them in Settings, Keyboard, and a setting there turns off the single keys that work anywhere, so yours may differ. The app always shows your own keys, and the rest of this guide does not repeat this. `Ctrl` means `Cmd` on a Mac. `g` then `i` means press `g`, let go, and press `i`.

Mila also has its own help, under Help in the sidebar. A question mark beside a name in the app opens the help for that part.
