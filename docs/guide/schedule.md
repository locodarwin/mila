# Dates, repeats and reminders

A task's Schedule section holds its start date, due date and time, how it repeats, and your reminders.

## Start and due dates

A task can have a start date, a due date, both, or neither. A due time can be added once there is a due date. Clearing the due date clears the time too.

Open the task and use its Schedule section. For a quicker way:

- Choose the date button on a task's row. It offers today, tomorrow, next week, a date you pick, no date, and Add to My Day.
- On a phone, swipe a row to the left for the same choices.
- Right-click a task and choose Schedule: Today, Tomorrow, Next week, No date, or Pick a date. See [Right-click menus](menus.md).
- To date several tasks at once, [select them](selecting-tasks.md#dates) and choose Schedule in the bar. Its A day later and A week later move each task's own dates.
- Type a date in [quick add](quick-add.md#dates).
- With a task focused or open, the [command palette](search-and-palette.md) has Due today, Due tomorrow and Clear the due date.
- Drag it on a [calendar or timeline](layouts.md).

Dates and times show as you chose in [Preferences](preferences.md), and due times follow the time zone shown in Settings, Notifications. Mila picks that zone up from your device.

## Repeats

Open the task and choose from its Repeat menu:

- Does not repeat
- Every day
- Every weekday
- Weekly, monthly or yearly on the task's due date (today, if it has none)
- Custom

Custom repeats every so many days, weeks, months or years. Weeks can fall on the weekdays you pick. Months can fall on a day such as the second Tuesday, or the last day.

You can also type a repeat in [quick add](quick-add.md#repeats), such as `every mon and thu`.

### On a schedule or after completion

A repeat keeps to its schedule unless you turn on Repeat after completion.

On a schedule, the next task is due on the next date the rule falls on. If you finish late, dates already gone are skipped, so you never get a pile of overdue copies.

After completion, the next task is due a set time after the day you finish this one. Use this for "water the plants 3 days after you last did".

### Completing a repeating task

Completing a repeating task, or moving it to a Done or Canceled status, keeps it as done and makes the next one just below it, with the new due date.

The next one keeps the title, notes, project, priority, due time, labels, people, custom fields and checklist, with the checklist unticked. Reminders tied to the due date come along too, moved to the new date.

Undo takes back the completion and the next task with it. If two devices complete the same task while offline, only one next task is made.

### Ending a repeat

In Custom, Until sets the last date the repeat may fall on. Completing the task after that makes no next one. To stop a repeat at once, choose Does not repeat.

## Reminders

A reminder tells you about a task at a moment you choose. Reminders are yours: nobody else sees the ones you set, and setting one tells nobody else anything.

Open a task and choose Add a reminder. What it offers depends on the task:

| The task has | You can pick |
|---|---|
| A due time | At the due time, 15 minutes before, 1 hour before, 1 day before, or the morning of the due date at 9:00 when it is due later that day |
| Only a due date | The morning of the due date at 9:00, or the day before at 9:00 |
| Anything | Tomorrow at 9:00, or any date and time you pick |

Choices already in the past are left out. You can also add one with [quick add](quick-add.md#reminders).

Reminders tied to the due date (at the due time, before it, or on the morning of it) move when the task's date or time changes, and come along when a repeating task comes back. Tomorrow at 9:00, and a date and time you pick, stay where they are.

[Notifications](notifications.md) says where reminders reach you. They always show in the app.
