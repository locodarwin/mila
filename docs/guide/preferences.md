# Preferences

Settings, Appearance holds how Mila looks for you, and Settings, Preferences how it behaves. These follow you to every device you sign in on. A few layout choices stay with each device, listed at the end.

## Appearance

These are on Settings, Appearance, above your own themes.

- Theme: Standard, Calm, Playful or High contrast, then the 22 made from Omarchy's, then any your workspace offers, then your own. See [Themes](themes.md).
- Scheme: System, Light or Dark. System follows your device.
- Density: Comfortable or Dense. Dense fits more rows on the screen.
- Font size: S, M, L, XL or XXL. Each step is about 12% larger, and rows and controls grow with the text.
- Accent: a hue for the app's accent color. A hue you pick here wins over a theme's hue. A theme whose accent was set by hand ignores it.

Your density and font size apply on top of any theme.

The [command palette](search-and-palette.md) also has Theme, Density and Font size commands, and Switch to light, Switch to dark and Follow the system light or dark.

## Dates and times

- Weeks start on: as this device does, Sunday, Monday or Saturday.
- Dates: as this device does, month first, day first, or ISO such as 2026-11-03.
- Times: as this device does, 12-hour or 24-hour.

Each choice shows what it looks like. Quick add's `next week` and the Time page's weeks follow your week start.

Emails and notifications use your date style too.

## Lists

Open Mila on chooses the page Mila opens on: Inbox, My Day, Upcoming or Projects. It applies when you open Mila fresh, never when you follow a link to a task.

Show completed tasks when a list opens has a list's Completed section open from the start.

## Task panel

Labeled boxes or Summary lines. See [The two layouts](tasks.md#the-two-layouts).

## Sidebar

Hide what you do not use, and put the rest in your order. The items are Inbox, My Day, Upcoming, Wiki, Time, Views and Projects. The arrow buttons move an item, as do `Alt+Up` and `Alt+Down`.

Guests do not see Wiki in the sidebar whichever way this is set.

## Keyboard

Shortcuts have a page of their own, Settings, Keyboard. See [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard.md).

## What stays with each device

These are remembered on each device, since a laptop and a wide monitor want different things:

- whether the sidebar is collapsed, its width, and whether its Views and Projects lists are folded
- the task panel's width, and whether it fills the page
- which summary lines are open in the task panel
- the wiki tree's and wiki page's widths
- which tab each project opens on
- a project's table layout and timeline zoom
- how Inbox, My Day and Upcoming are shown, filtered and arranged, for each workspace

Signing out on a device forgets these, along with the rest of what Mila kept in that browser. Your preferences above are kept on the server and come back when you sign in.

To resize the sidebar or the task panel, drag the edge between them, or focus the edge and use the arrow keys. `Shift` takes bigger steps, and `Home` and `End` go narrowest and widest.
