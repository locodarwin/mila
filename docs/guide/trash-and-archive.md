# The trash and the archive

The trash is for things you meant to delete. The archive is for finished projects you want to keep but no longer look at.

## The trash

Open it with Trash at the foot of the sidebar. The trash needs a connection.

Deleted projects, tasks, wiki pages and templates stay here for 30 days, then are gone for good. Each shows when it was deleted, with a Restore button.

- A deleted task or project takes what hangs off it along, and Restore brings that back.
- A wiki page deleted with the page above it comes back with that page, so only the page you deleted is listed.
- Restoring a page brings back its subpages, and the pages above it if those were deleted.

The trash shows the workspace you are in. A guest's trash shows only what was deleted in the projects shared with them.

Deleting from a [right-click menu](menus.md), or deleting several [selected tasks](selecting-tasks.md) at once, asks nothing: the message after it has Undo, and a deleted task, project or page goes to the trash as usual.

Comments, checklist items, files, labels, fields and saved views do not go to the trash. [Undo](offline-and-sync.md#undo) can take those deletes back. A deleted workspace does not go to the trash either. See [Renaming and deleting a workspace](people-and-roles.md#renaming-and-deleting-a-workspace).

## The archive

Archive, in a project's header or its right-click menu, takes the project out of the sidebar and lists, and keeps everything in it. A toast offers Undo.

Archived projects are listed under Archived on the Projects page. Open one to see it as usual, with a note at the top saying it is archived. Unarchive, in its header or its right-click menu, brings it back.

An archived project's tasks and wiki pages stay out of the palette, Upcoming, My Day's suggestions and saved views across projects. Open the project to reach them.

Its tasks stay quiet too: no reminders, no due notices, no "ready to start" notices, and nothing in the digest's due or over-estimate lists. Time you logged yesterday still shows in the digest, since that is a record of what you did. Comments and mentions on those tasks still reach you. Unarchive the project and its dates count again.
