# If you are a guest

Someone invited you to work with them on one or more projects. You have a Mila account, and in their workspace you see only what they shared with you.

## Getting in

Open the link in your invitation, type your name, and choose Join. It works for 7 days. After that, [signing in](signing-in.md) works with a link sent to your email, or with a passkey if you add one.

Your account also comes with a Personal workspace, yours alone. The menu at the top of the sidebar switches between it and the workspace you joined, and the invitation puts you in the shared one.

## What you can see

- The projects shared with you, with their tasks, statuses, and wikis.
- The workspace's labels, custom fields and shared saved views, as far as they apply to those projects.
- Names and pictures of the workspace's members and admins and owners.
- Other guests, only when you share a project with them.

## What you cannot see

- Other projects, the workspace's Inbox, or the workspace's own wiki.
- Anyone's email address. Other people's addresses are not sent to your device at all.
- The workspace's templates and automations.
- Guests who share no project with you.

A link to something you cannot open reads "a task" or "a page", without its title. Comments, task history and exports show people's names. Another guest you share no project with shows as "Someone".

The workspace may have automations, rules that change tasks when something happens to them, and what you do can start one. You see what a rule did, in a task's history and in a notification, but not the rule's name: it reads "an automation".

## What you can do

In the projects shared with you, you can work on tasks much as a member does:

- add tasks, edit them, complete them, delete them, and move them between your shared projects
- write notes and wiki pages, and add wiki pages
- add subtasks, checklists, files and comments
- set assignees, labels already made, dependencies between tasks you can see, and custom field values
- log time and run a timer
- pick tasks for My Day and set reminders, which are yours alone
- `@` mention anyone who can reach the task

The Trash shows what was deleted in your shared projects, and you can restore it.

You can also make a [calendar feed](calendar-feeds.md), an [export](exports-and-imports.md) and an [API token](api-and-webhooks.md) for what you can see, choose any [theme](themes.md) the workspace offers, and change your own [preferences](preferences.md) and [notifications](notifications.md).

The Time page shows only your own time.

## What you cannot change

The app leaves out what a guest cannot do, so you will not find these:

- Making a project, or renaming, archiving or deleting one, or editing its statuses.
- Adding a task to the Inbox. Guests have no Inbox, and going there takes you to Projects. Quick add asks you to name a project, such as `#alpha`, or to open one first.
- Adding a label, or a custom field.
- Email to task. Guests have no addresses for turning email into tasks.

If an edit is refused all the same, perhaps because a project was taken away from you while you were offline, Mila takes it back and says so in a message.

Importing a CSV file is not available to guests either. The page says so.

Changes you make to a shared saved view stay on your screen only. A note says they are not saved, and Reset puts the view back.

## If a project disappears

When someone takes a project away from you, it leaves your devices, along with its calendar events and your notifications about it. Your own edits made offline in that project, not yet sent, are refused.

If your role changes to member, you see every project.

## Leaving

Settings, General has Leave, with the workspace's name. Your account and Personal workspace stay. To remove them too, see [Deleting your account](account-and-backups.md#deleting-your-account).
