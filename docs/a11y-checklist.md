# Accessibility checklist

Mila targets WCAG 2.2 AA. Automated checks catch much of it; the rest needs a person with a keyboard and a screen reader. Run this list at the end of every milestone and record the results at the bottom.

## What the tests already check

Run on every build (`make test`), so a milestone only needs to confirm they are green:

- **axe** (`web/e2e/axe.ts`, used by every browser test): the WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA rules, plus the structure rules a screen reader user navigates by: one `main`, named and unique landmarks, all content inside a landmark, an `h1`, headings in order, named dialogs.
- **Where axe runs** (`web/e2e/a11y.e2e.ts` and others): sign-in (form and "Check your email"), setup, the passkey step, an invitation, a sign-in link that failed; Inbox, My Day with every section, Upcoming, Projects, Trash; Help, the shortcut reference and a help topic; a project as a list, a board, a calendar and a table, and on its Wiki tab; the workspace's wiki; a task with every field filled in, and its label and assignee menus open; every Settings page, Import and export and Admin included, and the CSV import with its columns and dry run; deleting an account with the address typed; a saved view with its filter open, and with its filter and Display panels open as a list and as a board; a guest's unsaved changes to a shared view; the command palette, quick add, the shortcut sheet, the workspace menu, the status editor, a project's Share dialog and the picture cropper while open; an info toast with Undo and an error toast; the collapsed sidebar; the schedule chooser; notifications; the API reference at `/api/docs`, signed out and signed in, with operations open and a request's response shown; Inbox, My Day and Upcoming as boards, Upcoming by day; a task's notes holding every kind of Markdown, each heading level among them; the Time page by week with its chart, with Log time open, and with a timer running (at the foot of the sidebar, or above the tabs on a phone); Settings two sections to a row on a wide screen (Profile, Preferences, Notifications with the weekly report turned on, Security, a workspace's General and Import and export). Settings, Appearance, and the theme editor with its color popover and its Problems open, on a theme of each built-in with the app in that character and with a custom theme in use, light and dark, desktop and phone; with a custom theme in use, a theme's More menu open, the import's dry run of a theme file, and on a phone the editor's preview sheet; Settings, Profile and Admin with a picture set (Remove picture); the style guide's Themes section. Light and dark, on desktop and phone, in the standard theme; the main screens, the wikis among them, again in Calm, Playful and High contrast, light and dark, on desktop, with Inbox, My Day and Upcoming as boards, the Time page by week and an open task's notes in Markdown; the style guide in all eight theme combinations.
- **Keyboard** (`web/e2e/keyboard.e2e.ts`, desktop): the skip link; every Tab stop on the main screens shows a focus indicator, is on screen and is not covered; a list and a board are one Tab stop each, walked with the arrows; opening a task puts focus in it and closing it (Escape, Close, Delete) puts focus back on the row, the calendar entry that opened it, or the next one; the completed list is one Tab stop too; Undo on a toast from the keyboard gives focus back and leaves later toasts free to go; fields in the task panel never hide under its sticky header; Escape closes only the innermost thing; moves with Alt and the arrows (list, board, calendar, sidebar projects) are announced; the palette's result count is said from inside the dialog, where a screen reader can still hear it. Every Tab stop also shows focus on a table, a timeline, Upcoming as a board (its resize edge included), the Time page, and Settings two sections to a row on a wide screen. The ? sheet opens with focus on its heading and shows the keys that work anywhere and on the focused row, and Escape puts focus back on the row; its All shortcuts link closes it on the way; G then H goes to Help and Ctrl+/ opens the sheet, but not from inside a field.
- **Help** (`web/e2e/help.e2e.ts`): the sidebar and the phone's top bar reach Help; searching it says how many results, and a result lands with focus on its section's heading; the palette finds help; a topic that is not there says so inside the app; topics open offline; quick add's Syntax legend opens and closes without losing the draft; going to Help again starts a new search; on a phone, a help link in the task sheet closes the task rather than opening the topic unseen under it; a sentence about a key that is off reads without it.
- **Templates** (`web/e2e/templates.e2e.ts`): axe passes with Save as a template open, on Settings, Templates with a template chosen, with New project from a template open, and with the New task menu open; the template list's buttons carry `aria-pressed`, the preview is a region named after the template.
- **Time** (`web/e2e/time.e2e.ts`, with the Time page in `a11y.e2e.ts` and `phone.e2e.ts`): axe passes with a task's Time section showing an estimate, entries and an entry being changed, and on the Time page; an estimate or a length that cannot be read is said in an alert beside its field; the palette's Log time on the task opens the form with focus in Time spent; the report's table has a caption, row and column headings, and scrolls sideways from the keyboard on a phone. By week, the chart is one stop whose arrow keys say each week's parts (End says the week under way, "so far"); axe passes with it showing, with a project's Time tab holding time on the project itself, and with the Time page's Log time open, which puts focus on Project, says a missing project in an alert, and gives focus back to Log time once logged.
- **Workspace themes** (`web/e2e/workspace-themes.e2e.ts`, desktop): axe passes with the Publish dialog open, on Settings, Workspace themes as an admin and as a member, and with the suggestion showing; a theme below the floors is a disabled radio button whose reason is its description; the suggestion and the notice of a theme no longer offered are regions named Theme suggestion and Theme no longer offered.
- **Timelines and dependencies** (`web/e2e/timeline.e2e.ts`, `web/e2e/dependencies.e2e.ts`): one Tab stop over the rows (a row's title button carries its dates and what it waits for); Alt+Left and Alt+Right move a task by the zoom's step and Alt+Shift with them its due date; Enter opens the task; a tray task is chosen to make it due today; the dependency search is a combobox whose options follow `aria-activedescendant`, Escape closes it back to its button and the task stays open; axe passes with the search open, and the timeline passes in both schemes on desktop and phone.
- **Tables** (`web/e2e/table.e2e.ts`, desktop; phone shows list rows): one Tab stop, the arrows walk the cells and headings; Left on a title folds its subtasks and Right shows them; Enter on a cell opens its picker with focus on the chosen choice, and Escape puts focus back on the cell; the list's keys (priority) work on a row's cells; Alt+Down moves a row in its group and Alt+Left moves a column with focus kept on its heading; Alt+Shift with Right and Left on a heading makes its column wider and narrower, said in pixels, with focus kept on it; headings carry `aria-sort`, and subtask rows `aria-level` and `aria-expanded`.
- **Wiki** (`web/e2e/wiki.e2e.ts`): New page puts focus in the title, and Enter goes on to the page; Alt with the arrows moves a page in the tree (up, down, under the page above and out a level); hiding the tree puts focus on Show pages, and Mod+. brings the tree back; the project's tabs move with Left and Right and keep focus on the tab chosen; on a phone the tree opens as a sheet; axe on the workspace's wiki and a project's.
- **Editor** (`web/e2e/editor.e2e.ts`, desktop): `/` at the start of a line opens a listbox the text points at with `aria-activedescendant`, which narrows as you type, Enter inserts and Escape closes it leaving the task open; a checklist's boxes are named by their text and are 24px; selecting text shows the Formatting toolbar, whose buttons say whether they are on (`aria-pressed`); Alt+F10 puts focus on the toolbar, Left and Right move along it, and Escape goes back to the text; the image picker is a dialog that passes axe; wide notes close with Escape without closing the task.
- **Wiki toolbar** (`web/e2e/wikitoolbar.e2e.ts`): the toolbar "Format and insert" in a page's bar has one Tab stop; Alt+F10 reaches it from the text when nothing is selected (the bar over selected words otherwise), arrows, Home and End move along it, Escape goes back to the text; its buttons say whether they are on (`aria-pressed`) and are dimmed with `aria-disabled` where they can't apply; Text style, Insert and More formatting open menus (radio items, items, checkbox items) that the keys enter at their first item, Up and Down move, and Escape closes back to the button; the folded buttons leave the row, and axe passes on desktop and on a phone, where the toolbar sits above the keyboard while the page has focus.
- **Links and history** (`web/e2e/links.e2e.ts`, desktop): `[[` opens a listbox named Link to a page or task that the arrow keys walk; a link followed opens its page or task; Linked from is a section with a heading; the history dialog lists versions as buttons, marks the chosen one with `aria-current`, and says Added and Removed before changed words; axe on a page with Linked from and on the history dialog.
- **Saved views** (`web/e2e/views.e2e.ts`): a view sorted, grouped by a field and showing it keeps all three after a reload; field conditions on a number, a date and a choice; on a sorted list Alt+Up says why it cannot move the row and moves nothing; grouped by label, a task under two labels is reached in each, J and K walk past both copies, and completing it leaves focus on another task; a view across projects grouped by priority says why Alt+Up cannot move a task, and grouped by project moves it; removing the last sort leaves focus on Add a sort, and Escape still closes the panel; on a board with priority columns Alt and the arrows set the priority; a guest's change shows the "Unsaved changes" note and Reset, and is never sent.
- **Inbox, My Day and Upcoming** (`web/e2e/listviews.e2e.ts`): axe passes on them as a table and as a board; on Upcoming's board, Alt with Right and Left moves a card to another day, said with the day, with focus kept on the card and nothing moved into Overdue, and the arrows alone walk the cards across the days; a board's resize edges are one Tab stop with their width as a value, and the arrow keys size the columns; the timeline's task names resize from the keyboard; a busy calendar day's entries are each reached with Tab and scrolled into view in their square, and the calendar passes axe.
- **API reference** (`web/e2e/api-docs.e2e.ts`): every tag is a heading; a link in the Endpoints list opens its operation and puts focus on it; Try it sends a request and shows the status, ETag and body in a region named for the request; signed out, it offers Sign in instead.
- **Phone** (`web/e2e/phone.e2e.ts`): every control on the main screens, a saved view with its panels open among them, the API reference with an operation open, the picture cropper, and the theme editor's preview sheet, its search, a theme's More menu and the import's dry run, Inbox, My Day and Upcoming as boards, the Time page with Log time open and by week, a project's Time tab with Log time open, Settings, Notifications and a workspace's General, People, Labels, Templates, Themes, Webhooks and Calendar pages, is at least 44px to a finger (a checkbox drawn small inside its label is hit through the label); the task sheet makes the page under it inert and holds focus.
- **Hover text** (`web/e2e/hovertext.e2e.ts`, desktop): everything that can be clicked says what it is when the pointer reaches it, on the main screens (a project as a list, a board, a calendar, a table and a timeline, its Wiki and Time tabs), Inbox, My Day and Upcoming as boards, the Time page with Log time open and by week, every Settings page, and the theme editor (a row open, a search, the whole app, the contrast problems).
- **Pictures** (`web/e2e/avatars.e2e.ts`): the cropper opens with focus on the picture, plus and the arrow keys are announced from inside the dialog, and Save leaves the picture in Settings; a drop and a paste open it too, and Escape cancels; a picture that cannot be fetched leaves the initials; offline, Upload picture and Remove picture are off and the hint says why; after Remove picture focus is on Upload picture, and after an admin removes one, on that row's Sign out everywhere.
- **Themes** (`web/e2e/themes.e2e.ts`, desktop; `keyboard.e2e.ts`): every built-in theme clears every contrast pair the editor checks, measured in the page; a theme whose text or focus ring is under 3:1 on the page cannot be used, and the theme in use keeps its last readable look; Fix clears a warning; Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z in the editor take back its own changes and never reach the app's undo; under reduced motion a theme's durations do not apply. From the keyboard alone: the parts of a theme are buttons with the shown one marked current, a setting's row opens in place with Enter, takes a hex and closes on Escape with focus back on the row; the search says how many settings it found, Tab goes on to them, and Escape in the field clears it; and a problem in the list takes focus to its setting. A click on the preview opens the setting that draws what was clicked, with focus on its row. The phone test measures the manager, the editor, a part of it and the color sheet.
- **Contrast in code** (`web/src/lib/theme/*.spec.ts`): accent text reaches 4.5:1 (7:1 in High contrast) on every surface for every hue; `--border-control` and the accent reach 3:1 against the surfaces controls sit on (WCAG 1.4.11).
- **Single-key shortcuts** (`web/e2e/preferences.e2e.ts`, `web/e2e/shortcuts.e2e.ts`): turning them off stops Q, /, ? and the G keys, and nothing then offers them (WCAG 2.1.4); a character key someone chose for a command that works anywhere is off too, and marked so in Settings, Keyboard, while keys with Ctrl or Alt keep working. Ctrl+/ still opens the shortcut sheet, which says so, and the shortcut reference in Help shows those keys as "Off" under a banner rather than offering a key that does nothing.
- **Changing shortcuts** (`web/e2e/shortcuts.e2e.ts`, desktop): a key recorded in Settings, Keyboard works, and the sidebar, the palette and the ? sheet show it; a key another command has is offered as a swap; reserved keys are refused and nothing is saved; Escape ends a recording without closing the open task, and Ctrl+K inside the recorder is recorded rather than opening the palette; a two-key sequence records and works; Reset all and its Undo; changed keys work offline. A key the theme editor's Undo or Redo always has (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+Y) is refused for a command that works anywhere or in the open task, saying why, except Ctrl+Z for the app's own Undo (`conflicts.spec.ts`). axe runs on the page while recording and with a conflict showing (`web/e2e/a11y.e2e.ts`).
- **Reports** (`web/e2e/reports.e2e.ts`): axe passes on every tab of the Reports page, on a saved report's own page, and with a report's chart in a wiki page; a focused chart steps through its points with the arrow keys and shows each, on the Reports page and inside a page, where the keys leave the page's text alone.
- **Importers** (`web/e2e/data.e2e.ts`): axe passes on each app's import screen with its dry run showing (GitHub Issues, the CSV apps, Notion, Confluence); after an import, focus is on the heading that says what came in.
- **Admin and invitations** (`web/e2e/admin.e2e.ts`, `web/e2e/accounts.e2e.ts`): axe passes on the Microsoft mailbox panel, not connected and showing a code, and on an invitation opened while signed in as someone else.

## By hand, each milestone

Use a keyboard only, then a screen reader: Orca with Firefox or Chrome on Linux, and VoiceOver on an iPhone with the installed app. Note anything that fails and file it before the milestone is called done.

### Keyboard only

1. Load Inbox. Tab once: "Skip to content" shows. Enter: the next Tab lands on "New task".
2. Add three tasks. Tab into the list, then Down and Up (and J and K) between rows. Tab moves through the current row's controls and then out of the list.
3. On a row: X completes (with an undo toast), T adds to My Day, 1 to 4 set priority, Alt+Up and Alt+Down move it, Delete deletes (undo from the toast).
4. Enter opens the task: focus is in the panel. Tab through every field to Comments and back with Shift+Tab; nothing is hidden under the header. Escape closes it and focus is back on the row.
5. In the panel, open the label menu and press Escape: the menu closes, the task stays open. Escape again closes the task.
6. Ctrl+K: type a project name, arrow past the visible results (the list scrolls), Enter goes there. Ctrl+K again, Escape: focus returns where it was.
7. On the project page, switch List, Board and Calendar with Tab and Enter. On the board, Tab reaches one card; arrows go between cards and columns; Alt+Right moves a card to the next column and focus stays on it.
8. In the calendar, Alt and the arrows move an entry by a day or a week; focus follows it.
9. Collapse the sidebar: every icon has a tooltip, and Tab still reaches everything.
10. Turn off single-key shortcuts in Settings, Keyboard: Q, / and G I do nothing; Ctrl+K still works. A character key given to a command that works anywhere is off too, and the editor says so in words beside the key.
11. In Settings, Keyboard, activate a key's button: focus goes to a field named "Press the keys for ...". Press a key with Alt: it is saved and focus is on its new button. For a command that works anywhere, a plain key waits: Enter keeps it alone, a second key makes a sequence. Escape cancels and focus goes back to the button; Tab and Shift+Tab leave the field and cancel (no keyboard trap). A key another command has shows a message, focus moves to its first button (Swap, Keep both, Try another key), and the message is read with it. Undo on the toast puts the old keys back.
12. Hover or tab onto a toast: it stays until you leave it.
13. With the OS asking for reduced motion, opening a task and moving between views only fade.
14. In Windows High Contrast (or Firefox's forced colors), focus shows as an outline everywhere.
15. In Settings, Import and export, choose a format with the arrow keys and export; when the export is ready its Download link is reached by Tab. Pick a CSV file in Import a CSV file: each column's "goes to" select is reached by Tab, Check the import is required before Import, and after importing focus is on the "Imported" heading.
16. On Settings, Profile, "Delete my account…" puts focus in the address field; the delete button stays off until the address matches.
17. In a shared workspace, a project's Share button opens the Share dialog with focus inside it; Space ticks a guest, Escape or Done closes it and focus goes back to the button. In Settings, Admin, each account's Admin switch and "Sign out everywhere" are reached by Tab, and signing out asks a second time.
18. On a row, press ?: the sheet opens with focus on its heading and shows Anywhere and "On a task in a list"; the arrow keys scroll it, Tab reaches All shortcuts, Help and Change shortcuts, and Escape puts focus back on the row. Ctrl+/ opens it too, also with single-key shortcuts off.
19. G then H opens Help. Type in Search help: the count is said; Enter opens the first result and focus lands on its heading. On a topic, "On this page" links move focus to their section; Back and Forward between sections do too.
20. On a saved view, Tab reaches Filter and Display; Enter opens each panel, every control in it is a native select, field, checkbox or button in Tab order, and Escape closes the panel with focus back on its button (an open task stays open). Add a sort, then change its key and direction with the arrow keys; the new order is announced ("Sorted by due date, soonest first"). Group by and Columns are announced too. In Filter, Add a field condition: its field, comparison and value are each reached by Tab, and Remove takes it away, with focus on the next condition's Remove or on Add a field condition.
21. On a sorted list, Alt+Up and Alt+Down move nothing and say "Choose manual order to move tasks in the list"; on a view across projects not grouped by project they say "Group by project to move tasks"; on a sorted board, Alt+Up and Alt+Down say the same about cards, and Alt+Left and Alt+Right still move a card to the next column.
22. As a guest on a shared view, change the sort: a region "Unsaved changes" follows the header in Tab order with a Reset button; the name cannot be edited and Share and Delete are not offered.
23. Open `/api/docs`. Tab reaches Mila, Sign in (signed out), then the Endpoints links (on a phone, the Endpoints fold first; Enter opens it). Enter on a link opens that operation with focus on its summary; Enter or Space on a summary opens and closes it. In Try it, every parameter and the body are labeled fields in Tab order and Send submits; the response region follows, and its body scrolls from the keyboard once focused.
24. In Settings, Profile, Tab reaches Upload picture (a file field behind a button-like label, its focus ring on the label) and, with a picture, Remove picture. Choosing a file opens "Crop your picture" with focus on the picture: the arrow keys move it (Shift for bigger steps), plus and minus zoom, the Zoom slider follows with the arrow keys, Tab reaches Cancel and Save, and Escape cancels with focus back on Upload picture. Offline, both buttons are off and the hint says a connection is needed. After Remove picture, focus is on Upload picture. In Settings, Admin, "Remove picture: <name>" is reached by Tab for an account with a picture, and once it is pressed focus moves on to that row's Sign out everywhere (or its Admin switch). While a picture is being saved, Cancel is off and Escape does nothing.

25. In Settings, Appearance, Tab reaches each theme's Use, Edit and More; More's items are buttons reached with Tab, and Escape closes it. New theme opens the editor. In the editor, Tab goes from Back to themes through the name, Contrast goal, Undo and Redo, Editing (arrow keys between Light and Dark), Problems, Use this theme and More, then the parts of the theme, then the settings of the part shown. Enter on a color opens its popover: Same as, Lightness, Chroma and Hue (each a slider with its number beside it), Hex and the color picker; Escape closes it with focus back on the color. With focus outside a text field, Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z undo and redo in the editor only. Problems moves focus to its list; Enter on a problem goes to its setting. On a phone, a part opens as a page of its own with All parts to go back, and Open preview shows the preview as a full-screen dialog Escape closes.

26. In a table, Tab reaches the grid once; the arrows go between cells, Home and End to the ends of a row. On a heading, Enter sorts (Shift+Enter adds a key) and Alt+Left and Alt+Right move the column; focus stays on it. On a cell, Enter opens its picker (Up and Down walk the choices, Enter picks, Escape closes back to the cell) and F2 renames the task; on a summary, Enter opens its choices. On a sorted table, Alt+Up and Alt+Down say to clear the sort.

27. On a wiki, Tab reaches the tree once, on the page open; Up, Down, Home and End walk it, Right opens a page's subpages and Left folds them, and Enter or Space opens the page. Alt with the arrows moves the page in the tree. The tree's buttons (New page, Hide pages) come before it, then the page's bar (Show pages when the tree is hidden, the path, Markdown and the page's menu), the title field and the page.

28. In a task's notes or a page, select text: the Formatting toolbar appears over it. Alt+F10 goes to it (Bold first), Left and Right move, Enter or Space applies, Escape goes back with the text still selected. Type `/` on an empty line: the menu opens, Up and Down choose, Enter inserts, Escape closes it and keeps the `/`. In a table, Tab goes to the next cell and from the last makes a row.

29. Type `[[` and a word: the menu of pages and tasks opens; Up and Down choose, Enter links, Escape closes it. In a page's menu, History opens a dialog: Tab reaches the versions, Enter shows one, then Restore this version and Close.

30. On a timeline, Tab reaches the rows once; Up, Down, Home and End walk them, Left folds a task's subtasks and Right shows them, Enter opens the task. Alt+Left and Alt+Right move the task by a day, week or month as the zoom says, and Alt+Shift with them its due date; each move is announced with the new dates, and a toast offers to move what waits for it when it now ends later. The zoom buttons and Today come before the rows, the Unscheduled tray after them.

31. In a task's panel, Add a task it waits for opens a search: typing narrows it, Up and Down choose, Enter adds the task and puts focus back on the button, Escape closes the search and keeps the task open. Each dependency's remove button names it ("Stop waiting for Drywall").

32. Save as a template opens a dialog with focus in it: the name field, Keep assignees, then Save template; Escape closes it and leaves the project or task as it was. On Settings, Templates, Tab reaches each template, then the preview's actions; New project from it opens its dialog, where the arrows choose a template and Tab reaches the name and the start date. The menu beside New task opens under its button, the arrows and Tab reach each template, and choosing one says what was made.

33. In a task's Time section, Tab reaches the estimate, Start a timer, Log time, then each entry's pencil (Change … logged by …), which opens the entry in place with focus on its form; Save, Delete and Cancel close it. Starting a timer is announced, and the running timer at the foot of the sidebar is a group named Your timer with its task and a Stop button that names the task. A timer left running 10 hours asks in a dialog with focus in it, its choices radio buttons; Later closes it until the next hour.

34. On Settings, Workspace themes for a shared workspace, Tab reaches each row's Use (or In use), then for an admin Suggest and More (Duplicate, Update from my theme, Rename, Unpublish), for a member Duplicate. Publish one of my themes opens a dialog with focus on the chosen theme: the arrows choose among the themes that can be published, Tab reaches the name and Suggest it to everyone, Escape closes it. The suggestion above the page has No thanks and Use it; using it is announced.

35. On Reports, the tabs are one Tab stop walked with Left, Right, Home and End; Tab then reaches Project, Period, Filter, Save as view, Time and Download CSV. Each chart is one Tab stop, walked with the arrows, and Show as table follows it. On a saved report's page, the same, without Project for a report of one project.

36. In a page, a report's chart is picked with a click or the arrows from the text above, and the formatting bar's Chart lists every chart as a button; Escape closes the bar. Tab into the block reaches the report's link and then the chart.

37. On an import screen, Tab reaches the file chooser, then where the pages or tasks go, the dry run, and Import; after the import, focus is on the heading that says what came in, and Open and Move them to the trash follow it.

38. On Settings, Admin with a Microsoft mailbox, Connect a Microsoft mailbox, Copy the code and Cancel are reached in turn; Disconnect asks inside the panel, with Disconnect and Keep it.

39. In Settings, Tab goes through the rail's pages section by section: You, then the Workspace picker and the picked workspace's two groups, then This Mila. On a workspace page, choosing another workspace with the arrow keys keeps to the same page where that workspace has it, or goes to General, and leaves the sidebar's workspace as it was. On a phone, Settings shows the rail alone, and each page starts with a Settings link back to it, and a workspace's page with the workspace's name.
40. In a wiki page with nothing selected, Alt+F10 goes to the toolbar's Text style; Left and Right move along it, Home and End to its ends, and Escape goes back to the text where the cursor was. Enter on Insert opens its menu at Heading; Up and Down move, Enter puts the block in and returns to the text, Escape closes the menu back to Insert. Narrow the window: the buttons that fold away are no longer in the arrow order, and More formatting holds them.

### Screen reader

1. Landmarks: Main navigation, main, Task details (when open), Settings navigation, and on a phone Running timer while one runs. Headings: one `h1` per page; status groups and My Day and Upcoming sections are `h2`; task panel sections sit under the task's title.
2. A task row reads its title, priority, labels, "2 of 3 subtasks done", comment count, repeat, reminders, "Assigned to …" and "Due …" or "Overdue, due …" on its schedule button.
3. A board card reads the same as its name and "Arrow keys go between cards. Alt and arrow keys move this one." as its description; after its move keys are changed in Settings, Keyboard, the description names the new keys.
4. Moving a row, a card or a calendar entry with the keyboard is announced ("Moved … to In progress, 1 of 3"). Changing a saved view's filter announces how many tasks match. The palette announces its result count.
5. Going offline and coming back is announced once each. Toasts are read as they appear; errors interrupt.
6. After "Email me a sign-in link", "Check your email" is read.
7. On the phone, the open task is the only thing reachable; swiping through it never reaches the list under it.
8. In Settings, Keyboard, a key's button reads "Q, shortcut for Add a task, change". The recorder is a text field, so Orca and NVDA switch to focus mode on their own and the keys pressed reach it; its instructions are read with it and say that a single key waits for Enter. After the first key for a command that works anywhere, "N. Press Enter to use N alone, or another key…" is announced. A saved key is announced ("Add a task: N."), and a conflict's message is read with the button focus moves to. Single-key shortcuts still compete with browse-mode quick keys, as before.
9. In the shortcut sheet and the reference in Help, each table is read with its caption ("On a task in a list"), each row starts with the action, and a key reads as words ("G then I", "Ctrl+K"), not as separate keycaps; a key that is off reads "Off". A help search result is a link named after its topic or section.
10. A saved view showing a custom field reads it on the row as "Size: Large" (a card the same, in its name). Sorting or grouping a view announces it ("Sorted by due date, soonest first", "Grouped by priority"); turning a field on or off announces "Size shown". A field condition on a deleted field reads "A deleted field" with a Remove button.
11. On `/api/docs`: landmarks are banner, Endpoints navigation and main; one `h1` ("Mila API"), each tag an `h2`, and Parameters, Body, Responses and Try it `h3`s inside an open operation. An operation's summary reads as a disclosure with its method, path and summary, collapsed or expanded. After Send, "The server answered 201 Created." is announced.
